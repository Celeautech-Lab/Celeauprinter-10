# Guide 04 — First print and operation

**CeleauPrinter 10 — Commissioning, calibration and first prints**

> **Status:** content plan. The full version of this guide will be published together with the project's assembly documentation. This file describes what the guide will cover; it does not yet contain a step-by-step procedure.

---

## About this guide

This booklet covers the **commissioning** of the CeleauPrinter 10: the checks that precede the first print, the initial settings, the first machine test and the maintenance routine that accompanies the first uses. It is written for the person who brings the machine into service and operates it day to day.

**How this guide relates to the previous guides:** the machine is already assembled, wired and configured at this point. This guide gathers what remains to be done **outside assembly**: initial settings, first print, troubleshooting and maintenance.

**Purpose of this document:** to explain the content that will be published in this guide once the project's assembly documentation is available. Calibration parameters, success criteria and detailed maintenance instructions will be specified in the final version of the booklet, at the same time as the previous chapters.

### Objectives

- Gather the **commissioning check points** coming from the three previous guides.
- Present the sequence of **initial calibration** and the order in which it will be detailed.
- Define the acceptance criteria for a **successful first print**.
- Provide a first-level **troubleshooting** method and a way to track the settings.
- Set up the **maintenance routine** to apply after the first prints.

## Prerequisites — check points before you start

Before opening this booklet, validate the following points:

- [ ] Guides **01 (mechanics)**, **02 (electronics)** and **03 (software)** have been read and their check points validated.
- [ ] The **wiring diagram**, the **nomenclature** and the **configuration files** for your version are available.
- [ ] The **printing material** is identified, compatible with the machine and stored in good condition.
- [ ] The **calibration tooling** provided by the project is ready, together with a means of measuring or visually checking the settings.
- [ ] The machine is **assembled, wired, configured and successfully tested with no material**.
- [ ] The **print area** is clear, ventilated if necessary, and the emergency stop is accessible.
- [ ] A **logbook** is ready to record the starting parameters and the successive settings.

## Plan of the booklet

The final version will be organised into the following chapters, in this order:

1. **Preparing the workshop and the machine** — installation, storage, cleaning, levelling of the machine and condition of the surfaces.
2. **Pre-start checks** — recap of the mechanical, electrical and software checks from guides 01 to 03.
3. **First heat-up** — controlled power-up, temperature monitoring and check that no anomaly appears.
4. **Initial calibration** — reference position, tool calibration, axes and any sensors, in the order that will be documented.
5. **No-material tests** — dry movements, control of noise, friction and positioning stability.
6. **First validation print** — choice of the test, starting parameters, monitoring and the acceptance criteria of the result.
7. **Iterative settings and logbook** — method for changing one parameter at a time, interpretation of common defects and traceability of the tests.
8. **Maintenance after the first prints** — cleaning, lubrication, visual inspection and preventive maintenance schedule.

## Safety and good practices

- Stay **present** during the first heat-up and the first prints: the machine has not yet proven itself.
- Keep **hands and tools away** from the moving parts and the hot surfaces.
- Check that there is **no material flying about** and plan suitable **ventilation** if the process generates it.
- Never **bypass a protection or a safety feature** to save time on a setting.
- Record the **settings** that are applied: without a written trace, a setting is neither passed on nor reproduced.
